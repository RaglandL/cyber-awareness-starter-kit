# **Risk Register and Ownership**

| Risk | What Could Happen? | Likelihood | Impact | Recommended Control |
|------|--------------------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Phishing text or QR code | An employee could enter login or payroll information on a fake website. | High | High | Train employees to verify unexpected requests and provide a clear reporting method. |
| Shared passwords | Someone could access a system without individual accountability. | Medium | High | Require unique accounts, strong passwords, and multifactor authentication. |
| Unlocked work devices | An unauthorized person could view or change workplace information. | Medium | Medium | Require screen locks and remind employees to lock devices before walking away. |



**Choose one risk from your register and answer these questions for yourself:**

## Chosen Risk: Phishing text or QR code

**Scenario:**

Jordan works in a warehouse. One morning, a supervisor receives a text message asking them to scan a QR code and “confirm” the team’s payroll information. Today, imagine Jordan reports it to the manager. The manager asks:
“Is one strange text really a serious risk?”


**1. What person or team should own this risk?**

IT/Security should own this risk because they understand how phishing scams work and can set security standards . IT/Security can create a control timeframe and procedure. Operations management should support IT/Security by creating policies regarding the handling of phishing texts and QR codes, when and where to report the texts, how to confirm the validity of the request. Payroll should be involved to verify that payroll is accurate.

**2. What proof would show that the recommended control is working?**

Once policy is created, a tracking showing at least 90% employees have completed the training. A  tracking that shows an increase in reported phishing texts or QR code requests, after the training.

**3. How often should the control be reviewed?**

The control should be reviewed monthly to verify the training compliance and the increase in reported texts. If the results are not as expected then we may need to update the control.
