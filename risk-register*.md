Cyber Awareness Risk Register

This risk register is for a fictional warehouse or retail workplace.

—
| Risk | What Could Happen? | Likelihood | Impact | Recommended Control |
|------|--------------------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Phishing text or QR code | An employee could enter login or payroll information on a fake website. | High | High | Train employees to verify unexpected requests and provide a clear reporting method. |
| Shared passwords | Someone could access a system without individual accountability. | Medium | High | Require unique accounts, strong passwords, and multifactor authentication. |
| Unlocked work devices | An unauthorized person could view or change workplace information. | Medium | Medium | Require screen locks and remind employees to lock devices before walking away. |



—
How Risks Were Rated
Likelihood describes how likely the event is to happen.

Impact describes how much harm the event could cause.

Risks rated high in both areas should receive quick attention.


Priority Recommendation
The organization should address phishing first because employees regularly receive texts and emails, and one successful attack could expose passwords, payroll information, or other sensitive data.
